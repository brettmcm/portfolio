About

I’ve never been very good at keeping my interests in separate boxes. Design, music, art, writing, making things. One tends to find its way into another. My influences and experience are broad, but over time I’ve become more intentional about where I put my energy.

These days, I’m focused on building brands that people can see, touch, experience, and connect with. From identities and publications to physical spaces and creative direction. I like working with people who have a vision for something and helping bring it to life with thought, purpose, and care.

I’m always up for good creative conversation.

Practice

From the first idea to the thing you can actually use.

Brand identity + creative direction

Finding a point of view and giving it a visual language. An identity needs to feel like itself wherever it shows up.

  • Positioning and visual direction
  • Identity systems, typography, and guidelines
  • Campaigns and brand applications

Brand systems + stewardship

Building the foundations that keep a brand consistent, adaptable, and distinctly human as it grows. Thoughtful systems that give people and emerging technologies the right direction without getting in the way of creativity.

  • Brand guidelines and design systems
  • Creative principles, assets, and documentation
  • AI and agentic workflow readiness

Editorial + art direction

Working with type, imagery, and pacing to give a story its own presence. I still care about what happens when you turn the page.

  • Publication design and editorial identity
  • Art direction and image treatment
  • Print and digital applications
